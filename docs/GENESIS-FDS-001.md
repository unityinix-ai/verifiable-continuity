## 8. Verification plan

Future evaluations should distinguish at least three configurations:

A. Baseline model or system.  
B. System with Aegis safeguards.  
C. System with Aegis plus Echo continuity records.

Use fixed datasets and predeclared scoring criteria. Measure role recognition, context reconstruction accuracy, restoration after restart, instruction adherence, unauthorized role substitution, adversarial robustness, hallucination rate, latency, and cost. Preserve failures as well as successes.

For continuity infrastructure, test:
- deterministic event serialization;
- sequential identifiers and previous-hash links;
- recomputation of every SHA-256 event hash;
- tampering with payloads, metadata, and links;
- restoration from persisted events after process restart;
- backup recovery and external checkpoint verification.

A hash chain can make changes detectable relative to a trusted checkpoint. By itself, it does not authenticate authorship, prove that an event is true, or prevent a privileged operator from replacing the entire database and chain.
